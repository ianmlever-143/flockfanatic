        How Flock Fanatic works

        Four Ravens a week. One jersey number each. Name the player.
        • Every Monday there are four Ravens: Easy (a household name), Medium (a solid contributor), Hard (an obscure name) and Expert (a guy who lasted a season or two).
        • You start with their jersey number and the years they played in Baltimore. Name them right there for the full 100.
        • Stuck? Reveal scouting hints in order. Each one costs 20 points.
        • Bonus: if you get the player without the college hint, name his college for 25 extra points.
        • You get three guesses per player, and each miss costs 10. A correct guess is always worth at least 5.
        • Sign in to post your score to the weekly leaderboard. Your first finished game counts.

        Your leaderboard name

        Use a nickname or a first name plus last initial, like "Ian L." Full names aren't allowed, for everyone's privacy.
